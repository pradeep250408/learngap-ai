Frequently Asked Questions

How does the adaptive algorithm work? ▼

The algorithm analyses each answer you submit, assigns a mastery score per concept, then selects the next question that targets the concepts with the lowest scores. It continuously updates in real‑time, focusing on the gaps that matter most.

Can I export my progress? ▼

Yes! Open the Progress page and click the "Export CSV" button. A file with your mastery scores, answered questions, and timestamps will be downloaded.

Is there a mobile app? ▼

We are currently building a responsive web app that works on any device. A native mobile app is on the roadmap for next quarter.
